you might be wondering.

Something else on your mind? Get in touch

Does it cost me anything?

Sending a garden enquiry through Tradesly is free. If you go ahead with a partner, you agree the work and its price directly with them. We charge partners for introductions; there is no homeowner matching fee.

Will several gardeners contact me?

We offer your enquiry exclusively to one matched garden partner. We don’t send it to a group of tradespeople. If a new introduction is needed, we’ll ask you first.

Is a gardener available in my area?

We’re starting with Bristol and nearby BS postcode areas. Availability depends on your job and the partners covering your area. We check this after you send a request; we can’t guarantee a match or a response time.

What happens after I send my request?

We review the job details and look for a suitable partner. We’ll contact you about the next step. If an introduction is possible, the partner can discuss the work with you and provide their own quote. A request doesn’t book any work.

What should I check before booking?

Ask the partner about relevant experience, insurance and examples of recent work. Agree the scope, price, payment terms and any dates in writing. Tradesly makes the introduction; the partner is the independent business carrying out the work.
