1. What Tradesly is (and isn't)

Tradesly is a quote-matching service. We review Bristol and nearby BS-postcode garden enquiries and, where a suitable partner is available, introduce the homeowner to one local garden partner. A request does not guarantee a match, quote or response time.

We are not:

  • A contractor or tradesperson — we don't do any work ourselves

2. Eligibility (homeowners)

To use Tradesly as a homeowner you must be:

3. Service is free for homeowners

Tradesly is and always will be free for homeowners. We do not charge you to request quotes, receive matches, or use any feature of the service. Tradespeople pay us a small matching fee for receiving your details — this never affects what they charge you.

4. The matching process

When you submit a quote request:

  • We match you with one suitable local garden partner covering your postcode
  • With your consent, we share your contact details and job description with one matched garden partner
  • The matched partner contacts you directly to discuss your job and quote
  • You decide whether to proceed after discussing the work and price with the partner
  • You contract directly with the tradesperson — Tradesly is not a party to that contract

8. How leads are priced & delivered

Leads are graded into four tiers based on qualification depth:

  • Tier 1 (Cold): garden enquiry submitted with basic details — £25
  • Tier 2 (Verified): homeowner confirms their interest — £45
  • Tier 3 (Quote-ready): timeframe and clarified project — £70
  • Tier 4 (Detailed): additional supporting project detail — £110

The initial service covers garden and landscaping jobs in BS postcode areas. Your tier acceptance, prices, coverage and delivery arrangements are agreed before paid introductions begin. A delivered lead does not guarantee a booking or a sale.

  • Lead is for work you don't cover

We review reported fake, duplicate or otherwise ineligible leads under these criteria. Approved disputes are excluded from billing or credited. Repeated disputes may trigger a review of the source or partner arrangement.

Disputes received after 24 hours are at our discretion.
